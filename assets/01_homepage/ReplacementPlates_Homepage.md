### Documents you'll need

Every registered supplier must see two things before making a plate:

**Proof of your name and address** — driving licence; utility, Council Tax or rates bill or bank/building society statement from the last 6 months; or national identity card. A passport or bank card proves your name only.

**Proof you can use the registration** — your V5C logbook or its green new-keeper slip, a V750 certificate of entitlement, a V778 retention certificate, a V11 tax reminder showing the registration, a V379 temporary registration certificate, a stamped V948 (physical authorisation) or an electronic eV948 confirmation, or a letter from a fleet or lease company quoting your V5C document reference number.

For the exact list and current guidance, see [GOV.UK: getting number plates made up](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/getting-number-plates-made-up). Read more: [documents needed to buy number plates](/guides/documents-needed-to-buy-number-plates).

## Plates made to the legal requirements

Every plate we make is produced to the legal requirements for number plates: the correct Charles Wright characters, correct spacing, the right reflective background (white front, yellow rear), solid black non-reflective characters, and the required supplier identification and British Standard marking.

These rules cover how a plate must be made and displayed. They're separate from what a DVSA MOT tester checks — the MOT inspection looks at things like whether characters are correctly formed, evenly spaced, secure and not obscured; it does not check for the supplier's name, postcode or BS number on the plate. A correctly made plate can still fail an MOT if it's damaged, dirty, insecurely fitted or otherwise not properly displayed — meeting the legal requirements and passing an MOT are related but not the same thing.

Our Standard, 3D, 4D, 5D and Bevel styles are made to these requirements. Ghost's specific construction and compliance information is being finalised — see the [Ghost product page](/ghost-number-plates) for its current status before ordering that style.

## FAQs

**How quickly can I get replacement number plates?**
For delivery: order before 2pm on a working weekday and, once your documents are checked, we aim to dispatch by Royal Mail the same day. For collection: ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling.

**Where do I collect from?**
Our Ilford collection point, IG1 3QF. Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling.

**Do you have shops in other towns?**
No — one collection point, in Ilford. Elsewhere, we deliver by Royal Mail (see [delivery](/delivery) for areas).

**Do I need documents?**
Yes. UK law requires proof of your name and address, and proof you're entitled to use the registration.

**Can I replace just one plate?**
Yes. Order a single front or rear plate and tell us the size and style of the one you're keeping.

**My plates were stolen. What should I do?**
Report it to the police and keep the reference number, then order replacements. If you later get fines for journeys you didn't make, your registration may have been cloned — tell the police and whoever issued the fine.

**Can I change style when I replace my plates?**
Yes — go from Standard to 3D, 4D, 5D, Ghost or Bevel. The registration, typeface, spacing and markings stay the same; only the finish changes.

**Are the prices per plate or per pair?**
Per plate. A "pair" means two plates — front and rear — at the pair price shown on each product page.

**What if my plate has a manufacturing fault?**
Contact us and we'll assess it.
