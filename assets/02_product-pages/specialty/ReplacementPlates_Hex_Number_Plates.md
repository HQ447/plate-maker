# Hex Number Plates

Hex number plates start from a standard plate with the corners cut away to a hexagon-style outline. Each one is made to order. Enter your registration in the builder to see your plate and the price before you pay.

[ Enter your registration ] [ Build my hex plate ]

## Hex V1 and Hex V2

We offer two hex profiles, **Hex V1** and **Hex V2**, which cut the plate outline differently. The builder previews each on your registration before you order.

## How a hex plate is made

A hex plate is made from a standard plate blank by cutting away the corners and edges to the chosen profile. Because it is cut for your registration, hex plates are made to order in custom sizes rather than one fixed size.

## What the rules require

Number plate rules apply to a hex plate as to any plate: reflective material, the right colours, characters of the required size, font and spacing with the required margins, and the supplier's name and postcode and the British Standard number on the plate. The shape of the finished plate has to leave all of that intact. See [legal number plates](/legal-number-plates).

## Replacing a hex plate

- **Cracked or damaged:** order a replacement in the same profile.
- **Matching a plate you already have:** tell us which profile you have. We can't guarantee an identical match to a plate made by another supplier, because cut profiles vary.
- **Only one plate:** you can order a single front or rear plate.

## Warranty

New orders carry a manufacturing-defect warranty that follows the finish you choose: 6 months for Standard, 3D Gel and 4D, and 12 months for 5D, Ghost and Bevel, from the delivery or collection date, in addition to your statutory rights. A warranty covers manufacturing defects; it is not a statement that a product is approved for road use. See [warranty and faulty plates](/warranty).

## Documents you'll need

**Proof of your name and address** — driving licence; utility, Council Tax or rates bill or bank/building society statement from the last 6 months; or national identity card. A passport or bank card confirms your name only, so it needs a document showing your address alongside it.

**Proof you can use the registration** — for example a V5C or its green new-keeper slip, a V750 or V778, a V11, a V379, a stamped V948 or an eV948, or a fleet or lease company letter quoting the V5C document reference number.

See [documents you need](/documents-you-need) and [GOV.UK: getting number plates made up](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/getting-number-plates-made-up).

**What's the difference between Hex V1 and Hex V2?**
They are two different cut profiles for the plate outline. The builder previews both on your registration.

**Are hex plates a fixed size?**
No. They are made to order in custom sizes from a standard blank.

**Can I order just one hex plate?**
Yes: a single front or rear plate, or a pair.
