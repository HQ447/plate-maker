# Japanese Import Number Plates

Number plates for imported vehicles whose plate-fixing area is too small for a standard plate. Each is made to order. Enter your registration in the builder to see your plate and the price before you pay.

[ Enter your registration ] [ Build my import plate ]

## Which vehicles can use the smaller layout?

Regulation 14A of the Road Vehicles (Display of Registration Marks) Regulations 2001 allows a smaller layout for an imported vehicle only where **both** of these are true:

- the vehicle does not have European Community Whole Vehicle Type Approval; and
- it is so constructed that the area available for fixing the plate means a registration mark cannot be displayed in the standard layout.

Being imported, or Japanese, is not enough on its own. Whether you can use the smaller layout depends on whether the required standard character layout can fit in the space, not just on whether one blank fits. A common standard car-plate size is 520mm × 111mm; if your vehicle can display the standard layout, use a standard plate: see [standard number plates](/standard-number-plates).

## The Regulation 14A layout

| Measurement | Requirement in regulation 14A(2) |
|---|---|
| Character height | 64mm |
| Character width (except 1 and I) | 44mm |
| Stroke | 10mm |
| Spacing between characters within a group | 10mm |
| Vertical spacing between groups (lines) | 5mm |
| Margin between the mark and the top and sides of the plate | not less than 5mm |
| Space between the bottom of the mark and the bottom of the plate | not less than 13mm; within that space, not less than 5mm between the bottom of the mark and the top of the supplier's name and postcode |

On two lines the layout needs a minimum of 151mm of height (5mm + 64mm + 5mm + 64mm + 13mm). That is a minimum-layout calculation, not approval of any particular plate size or product. The width, the number of characters and the supplier's name and postcode must also fit, and each finished plate has to be checked. Source: [Regulation 14A as inserted by SI 2002/2687](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2687/pdfs/uksi_20022687_en.pdf).

## Check your recess and your vehicle

1. Measure the width and height of the **space** where the plate sits, not the old plate.
2. Check the surface is flat and clear of trim or sensors, and measure front and rear separately.
3. Tell us your vehicle and the recess size so we can check that it qualifies and that the finished layout works before we make your plate.

The builder shows the sizes offered for your registration.

## The rules that still apply

A plate on a qualifying vehicle must still be reflective, black on white at the front and black on yellow at the rear, carry the supplier's name and postcode and the British Standard number, and show the registration correctly. See [legal number plates](/legal-number-plates).

## Replacing an import plate

- **Original lost, cracked or faded:** order a replacement in the size that fits your recess.
- **Plate from another supplier:** measure it and the recess and contact us.
- **One plate only:** order a single front or rear plate.

## Warranty

New orders carry a manufacturing-defect warranty that follows the finish you choose: 6 months for Standard, 3D Gel and 4D, and 12 months for 5D, Ghost and Bevel, from the delivery or collection date, in addition to your statutory rights. A warranty covers manufacturing defects; it is not a statement that a product is approved for road use. See [warranty and faulty plates](/warranty).

## Documents you'll need

Your imported vehicle must be registered with the DVLA, and the usual documents apply.

**Proof of your name and address** — driving licence; utility, Council Tax or rates bill or bank/building society statement from the last 6 months; or national identity card. A passport or bank card confirms your name only, so it needs a document showing your address alongside it.

**Proof you can use the registration** — for example a V5C or its green new-keeper slip, a V750 or V778, a V11, a V379, a stamped V948 or an eV948, or a fleet or lease company letter quoting the V5C document reference number.

See [documents you need](/documents-you-need) and [GOV.UK: getting number plates made up](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/getting-number-plates-made-up).

## Japanese import number plate questions

**Is JDM the same as Japanese import?**
People use both terms for plates for Japanese-market imports.

**Can any imported car use a smaller plate?**
Only a vehicle that meets the Regulation 14A conditions above.

**Do I need a front and rear?**
Cars need both. You can order a single front or rear plate or a pair.

**How do I collect my plate?**
Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling.
