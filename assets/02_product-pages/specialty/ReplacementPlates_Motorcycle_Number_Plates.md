# Motorcycle Number Plates

Rear number plates for motorbikes and scooters, made to order in the two-line layout motorcycles use. Enter your registration in the builder to see your plate and the price before you pay.

[ Enter your registration ] [ Build my motorcycle plate ]

## Do I need a front number plate on my motorbike?

- **Registered on or after 1 September 2001:** display a number plate at the **rear only**.
- **Registered before 1 September 2001:** you can also display a front plate, but you don't have to.

Motorcycle and motor tricycle characters (registered on or after 1 January 1973) are set on **two lines**. Source: [GOV.UK rules for number plates](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/rules-number-plates).

## Motorcycle plate layout

| Measurement | Requirement |
|---|---:|
| Character height | 64mm |
| Character width (except 1 and I) | 44mm |
| Stroke | 10mm |
| Horizontal space between characters | 10mm |
| Vertical space between the two lines | 13mm |
| Margins (top, bottom, sides) | at least 11mm |

Source: DVLA leaflet INF104.

### How big does the plate have to be?
The plate must be big enough to hold that layout. Our own arithmetic from those figures: two 64mm lines with a 13mm gap between them and 11mm above and below need at least **163mm of height**. A four-character top line needs about **228mm of width** (176mm of characters, 30mm of gaps between them and 22mm of side margins), less if it contains narrow 1 or I characters. This is our worked example, not an official table. The sizes offered for your registration are shown in the builder.

Motorcycles registered before 1 September 2001 may be covered by earlier rules. Contact us before ordering for an older machine.

## Replacement motorcycle number plates

- **Cracked or damaged:** a broken or delaminated plate can be hard to read and may fail an MOT. Order a replacement rear plate.
- **Faded or worn:** if the plate has lost contrast or reflectivity, it is due for replacement.
- **Lost or stolen:** if a plate is stolen, tell the police first, then order a replacement. See [stolen number plates and vehicle cloning](/guides/stolen-number-plates-and-vehicle-cloning).
- **Changing finish:** the builder shows the finishes offered for motorcycle plates.

## What the rules require

Plates must be made from reflective material, with black characters on a yellow background at the rear, characters of the right size, spacing and font, and marked with the supplier's name and postcode and the British Standard number (BS AU 145e for plates fitted after 1 September 2021). If fitted after that date the characters must be a single shade of black. Small "show" plates without the required markings are not for road use. See [legal number plates](/legal-number-plates).

## Warranty

New orders carry a manufacturing-defect warranty that follows the finish you choose: 6 months for Standard, 3D Gel and 4D, and 12 months for 5D, Ghost and Bevel, from the delivery or collection date, in addition to your statutory rights. A warranty covers manufacturing defects; it is not a statement that a product is approved for road use. See [warranty and faulty plates](/warranty).

## Documents you'll need

**Proof of your name and address** — driving licence; utility, Council Tax or rates bill or bank/building society statement from the last 6 months; or national identity card. A passport or bank card confirms your name only, so it needs a document showing your address alongside it.

**Proof you can use the registration** — for example a V5C or its green new-keeper slip, a V750 or V778, a V11, a V379, a stamped V948 or an eV948, or a fleet or lease company letter quoting the V5C document reference number.

See [documents you need](/documents-you-need) and [GOV.UK: getting number plates made up](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/getting-number-plates-made-up).

**How many number plates does my motorbike need?**
One at the rear if it was registered on or after 1 September 2001. Older machines may also show a front plate but don't have to.
