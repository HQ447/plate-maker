# Oversized Number Plates

Oversized rear number plates for vehicles whose rear plate recess is larger than a standard plate. Each is made to order. Enter your registration in the builder to see your plate and the price before you pay.

[ Enter your registration ] [ Build my oversized plate ]

## Oversized plate, not oversized characters

An oversized plate has a **larger blank**. The **characters** are not made larger: their size, stroke and spacing follow the rules that apply to your vehicle and plate. The extra plate area is simply plate. A common standard car-plate size is 520mm × 111mm; the builder shows the oversized rear sizes offered for your registration.

Source for character rules: DVLA leaflet INF104. See [legal number plates](/legal-number-plates).

## Measure your rear plate recess

1. Measure the **width and height of the recess** where the rear plate sits.
2. Check that the plate would sit flat, without covering trim, lights or sensors.
3. Compare with the sizes in the builder. If yours falls between sizes, contact us before ordering.

Please don't trim a plate to make it fit: trimming can remove required margins and markings.

## Replacing an oversized rear plate

- **Cracked, faded or delaminated:** order a replacement in the same size.
- **Lost or stolen:** if stolen, tell the police first, then order a replacement.
- **Rear only:** you can order a single rear plate.
- **Front plate:** if you're not sure what fits at the front, [contact us](/contact).

## Warranty

New orders carry a manufacturing-defect warranty that follows the finish you choose: 6 months for Standard, 3D Gel and 4D, and 12 months for 5D, Ghost and Bevel, from the delivery or collection date, in addition to your statutory rights. A warranty covers manufacturing defects; it is not a statement that a product is approved for road use. See [warranty and faulty plates](/warranty).

## Documents you'll need

**Proof of your name and address** — driving licence; utility, Council Tax or rates bill or bank/building society statement from the last 6 months; or national identity card. A passport or bank card confirms your name only, so it needs a document showing your address alongside it.

**Proof you can use the registration** — for example a V5C or its green new-keeper slip, a V750 or V778, a V11, a V379, a stamped V948 or an eV948, or a fleet or lease company letter quoting the V5C document reference number.

See [documents you need](/documents-you-need) and [GOV.UK: getting number plates made up](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/getting-number-plates-made-up).

## Oversized number plate questions

**Does oversized mean bigger characters?**
No. Characters keep the size the regulations require; the extra size is the plate.

**Will an oversized plate fit my vehicle?**
Measure your rear plate recess and compare with the builder. If you're not sure, contact us before ordering.

**Can I get an oversized plate in 3D or 4D?**
