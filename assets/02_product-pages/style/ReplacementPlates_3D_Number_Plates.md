## What Are 3D Gel Number Plates?

3D number plates — also called 3D gel or domed resin plates — have raised characters built up from polyurethane resin over solid black characters on a reflective acrylic plate.

They're sometimes confused with an older style printed flat with two-tone shading to *look* raised. Plates fitted since 1 September 2021 must use a single shade of black, so that shaded-print style isn't permitted. Genuine raised 3D gel characters in solid black are a different product.

## Are 3D Number Plates Legal in the UK?

Yes, when made correctly: solid black, non-reflective characters in the Charles Wright typeface at the legal size and spacing, on the correct reflective background, with the required supplier and British Standard markings. Raised characters are permitted under the current rules.

This covers how the plate must be made and displayed. It's a separate question from what a DVSA MOT tester checks (see below).

## 3D Number Plate FAQs

**Is the £19.95 price per plate or for a pair?**
Per plate. A pair (front and rear) is £39.90.

**Are 3D gel number plates legal in the UK?**
Yes, when made correctly — see "Are 3D Number Plates Legal" above.

**Can I replace just one 3D plate?**
Yes. Order a single front or rear plate in the size of the one you're keeping.

**Can you match a 3D plate made by another supplier?**
We'll match the size and use our 3D gel finish, but an exact visual match to a different manufacturer's plate isn't guaranteed — gel depth and finish vary between suppliers.

**Are raised characters legal, or only printed ones?**
Raised characters are permitted, provided the plate meets the size, spacing, colour and marking rules — see above.

**Can I get 3D gel on a short or motorcycle plate?**
Short sizes are listed above. For motorcycle, choose "motorcycle" in the builder to see current 3D options for your registration.

**Do I need new documents if I'm putting on a newly assigned private registration?**
Whether you need a fresh entitlement document depends on whether the registration has just been assigned or retained to your vehicle — check your V5C or retention certificate (V778) matches what you're ordering against.

**Can I collect the same day?**
Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling.

**Is the Tracked 24 upgrade worth it?**
It adds tracking and Royal Mail's own next-working-day delivery aim, subject to their service terms — useful if you want visibility on your order's progress.

**Will fitting a 3D plate damage the gel?**
Handle the plate by its edges and use the fixing method suited to your car (sticky pads or screws); avoid pressing directly on the raised characters.

**What if my plate has a fault?**
Contact us and we'll assess it.
