## Diamond-Cut Number Plates — The Bevelled Edge Explained

Bevel — also called bevelled or diamond-cut — characters are acrylic, cut with an angled edge rather than a flat or domed one. The angled edge catches light differently depending on the viewing angle, giving a faceted look. This is our highest-priced style.

## Are Bevelled Number Plates Legal?

Yes, when made correctly: solid black, non-reflective characters at the legal size and spacing, on the correct reflective background, with the required supplier and British Standard markings. This is separate from the DVSA MOT check, which looks at condition, legibility, security and fitting.

## Bevel Number Plate FAQs

**Is bevel the same as diamond-cut?**
Yes — different names for the same angled-edge finish.

**Is £39.95 the price per plate or per pair?**
Per plate. A pair is £79.90.

**How is bevel different from 4D or 5D?**
4D has a flat-topped acrylic edge; 5D adds a gel top; bevel is cut with an angled, faceted edge instead.

**Can I add a UK, EV or other badge to a Bevel plate?**
If your vehicle is eligible, choose the badge option in the builder alongside your Bevel plates.

**Can you match another supplier's bevel plate?**
We'll match it to our own profile; an identical match to a different manufacturer isn't guaranteed.

**Do you offer bevel in short or hex formats?**
The builder shows the sizes and options offered for your registration. Bevel describes the character edge, not the plate's shape.

**Are bevelled plates legal?**
Yes, when made to the current rules — see above.

**Is the price inclusive of delivery?**
The plate price is separate from delivery. First Class is £3 on orders under £15 and free from £15, and Tracked 24 is an additional £2; see the delivery section above.

**Can I collect the same day?**
Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling.

Yes. The same delivery options and charges apply across every style.
