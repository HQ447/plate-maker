## Standard, 2D and Printed Number Plates Explained

A standard number plate — also called 2D or printed — has flat characters printed directly onto a reflective acrylic plate, with no raised or domed finish. It's the simplest and least expensive of our styles, and it meets exactly the same legal requirements for character size, spacing, colour and markings as our raised-character styles.

## Standard Plate Sizes and Reflective Acrylic Construction

Standard plates are made on the same reflective acrylic base as our other styles — white reflective at the front, yellow reflective at the rear — with printed rather than raised characters.

A common standard car-plate size is 520mm × 111mm. Other sizes may be available for some registrations; the builder shows what is offered for this style once you enter your registration.

### Documents for your registration
**Proof of your name and address** — driving licence; utility, Council Tax or rates bill or bank/building society statement from the last 6 months; or national identity card. A passport or bank card proves your name only.

**Proof you can use the registration** — your V5C logbook or its green new-keeper slip, a V750 certificate of entitlement, a V778 retention certificate, a V11 tax reminder showing the registration, a V379 temporary registration certificate, a stamped V948 (physical authorisation) or an electronic eV948 confirmation, or a letter from a fleet or lease company quoting your V5C document reference number.

For the exact list and current guidance, see [GOV.UK: getting number plates made up](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/getting-number-plates-made-up). Read more: [documents needed to buy number plates](/guides/documents-needed-to-buy-number-plates).

## Road-Use Requirements for Standard Plates

Standard plates meet the same legal requirements as every style we make: correct Charles Wright characters, correct spacing, the right reflective background, solid black non-reflective characters, and the required supplier and British Standard markings. This is separate from the DVSA MOT check, which looks at condition, legibility, security and fitting rather than the supplier markings.

## Standard Replacement Plate FAQs

**Is £12.49 the price for one plate or a pair?**
One plate. A front-and-rear pair is £24.98.

**Can I order just a rear, or just a front, plate?**
Yes — choose front, rear or both in the builder.

**What's the difference between "standard" and "2D"?**
Same product — flat, printed characters, as opposed to a raised 3D, 4D, 5D, Ghost or Bevel finish.

**What's the plate made from?**
Reflective acrylic, with the registration printed directly onto it.

**Do you offer fixing pads or screws?**
Where available, you'll see fixing options at checkout.

**Can I collect the same day?**
Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling.

**Is the Tracked 24 upgrade worth it for a standard plate?**
It adds tracking and Royal Mail's own next-working-day aim — the same option applies whichever style you order.

**Can I order a plate for a newly assigned private registration?**
Yes, with the matching entitlement document — your V5C or retention certificate showing that registration.

**I need a plate for a trailer — can it be a standard plate?**
A trailer must display the same number plate as the vehicle towing it. Order the plate in the size that fits your trailer; see [bike rack and trailer number plates](/guides/bike-rack-and-trailer-number-plates-different-rules).

**My plate was lost or stolen — what next?**
Report a theft to the police, keep the reference number, then order a replacement. See our guidance on [lost](/guides/lost-number-plate-what-to-do-next) and [stolen](/guides/stolen-number-plates-and-vehicle-cloning) plates.
