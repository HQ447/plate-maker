# About ReplacementPlates

ReplacementPlates makes number plates to order for drivers who need a replacement: a cracked, faded, lost or stolen plate, a single plate to go with one you're keeping, or a change of finish.

## Who we are

ReplacementPlates is a trading name of **Private Number Plate Maker Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16163051). We are a DVLA-registered number plate supplier, supplier ID (RNPS) 75449. The company has sold **12,000+ plates since January 2025**.

The RNPS number identifies us as a registered supplier. It does not mean the DVLA has approved any particular plate design; each plate still has to meet the legal requirements.

## What we make

[Standard](/standard-number-plates), [3D gel](/3d-number-plates), [4D](/4d-number-plates), [5D (4D gel)](/5d-number-plates), [Ghost](/ghost-number-plates) and [Bevel](/bevel-number-plates) plates, plus [motorcycle](/motorcycle-number-plates) and [oversized](/oversized-number-plates) formats. Prices are per plate, and you can order a single front or rear plate or a pair. See [prices](/prices).

## How we work

- **Documents first.** The law requires a registered supplier to check your identity and your right to use the registration before supplying road-use plates. See [documents you need](/documents-you-need).
- **Marked plates.** Road-use plates carry the supplier's name and postcode and the British Standard number.
- **Cancel before production.** You can cancel for a full refund at any time before production starts, with no fee. See [returns and cancellations](/returns).
- **Warranty.** New orders carry a manufacturing-defect warranty of 6 months for Standard, 3D Gel and 4D, and 12 months for 5D, Ghost and Bevel, from the delivery or collection date, in addition to your statutory rights. See [warranty](/warranty).
- **Clear about locations.** We have one collection location, in Ilford, and deliver by Royal Mail. We don't have shops or branches elsewhere. See [delivery](/delivery) and [collection in Ilford](/number-plates-ilford).
