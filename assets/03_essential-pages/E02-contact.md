# Contact ReplacementPlates

The quickest way to reach us is by WhatsApp or telephone. You can also email us.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Email** | [info@replacementplates.uk](mailto:info@replacementplates.uk) |
| **WhatsApp** | +44 7754 185366 |
| **Telephone** | 020 3576 6603 |
| **Post** | Private Number Plate Maker Ltd, Stand 53, New Spitalfields Market, 1 Sherrin Road, London, E10 5SQ |

When you contact us, please give your **order number** if you have one and, for a question about a plate, your registration and what the problem is. A photo helps if a plate is damaged or wrong, but isn't required.

## Collecting your plates

Our collection location is **Castleview Gardens, Ilford, IG1 3QF**. Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling. We give exact arrival instructions by WhatsApp when we confirm your collection. See [collection in Ilford](/number-plates-ilford).

We don't have shops or branches elsewhere. See [delivery](/delivery).

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel for a full refund at any time before production starts. No cancellation or administration fee applies. Once production has started, personalised plates cannot normally be cancelled for a change of mind. Your statutory rights are unaffected. Contact [info@replacementplates.uk](mailto:info@replacementplates.uk), telephone 020 3576 6603 or WhatsApp +44 7754 185366 as soon as possible.

## Post and company details

ReplacementPlates is a trading name of Private Number Plate Maker Ltd (company number 16163051). Our registered office is Stand 53, New Spitalfields Market, 1 Sherrin Road, London, E10 5SQ. This is our address for post. Collections are not made from it, and please don't send plates back unless we ask you to.

## Making a complaint

If something has gone wrong, tell us by email, WhatsApp, telephone or post. We'll acknowledge your complaint, look into it and tell you what we can do. See the Complaints section of our [terms](/terms). Complaints about how we handle your personal data are covered in the Complaints section of our [privacy policy](/privacy).

## Data protection requests

To ask about or exercise your rights over your personal data, contact us as above. See our [privacy policy](/privacy).
