# Delivery and Dispatch

## Delivery charges

| Goods order value | Royal Mail First Class | Royal Mail Tracked 24 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Under £15 | £3 | £5 total delivery charge |
| £15 or more | Free | £2 total delivery charge |

Standard Royal Mail First Class delivery is £3 on orders under £15 and free on orders of £15 or more. Upgrade to Royal Mail Tracked 24 for an additional £2. Royal Mail delivery times are aims, not guarantees.

An order of exactly £15 qualifies for free First Class delivery. Order value means the price of the goods in your order, before delivery. Tracked 24 is £2 more than First Class in every case: £5 in total on orders under £15, and £2 in total on orders of £15 or more.

The delivery charge that applies to your order is shown next to our prices and in your basket, with a running total that includes it. Royal Mail Tracked 24 is optional and shown separately. If you choose to collect your plates from Ilford, no delivery charge applies.

Royal Mail's delivery aims: First Class aims for the next working day, including Saturday; Tracked 24 aims for the next working day with tracking. Both are subject to Royal Mail's service terms.

## Dispatch, delivery and collection are different

**Dispatch** is when we hand your parcel to Royal Mail. **Delivery** is when Royal Mail brings it to you. **Collection** is when you pick up your plates from us.

### When we aim to dispatch
Your plates are made to order and we can only supply them once the required document checks are complete. If your order **and** those checks are complete before 2pm on a working weekday (Monday to Friday), we aim to dispatch it that day. If they are completed later than that, or at a weekend, we don't aim for same-day dispatch.

### What can delay dispatch
A missing or unclear document, or a registration that doesn't match the vehicle evidence, can delay checks. See [documents you need](/documents-you-need).

### Examples
- **Monday to Thursday, complete before 2pm:** we aim to dispatch the same day; Royal Mail then aims to deliver the next working day.
- **Friday, complete before 2pm:** we aim to dispatch the same day. First Class includes Saturday delivery, but your parcel may arrive later, including the following week. We don't promise a Saturday or Monday arrival.
- **Bank holidays:** Royal Mail doesn't normally deliver on bank holidays, so allow extra time around them.

## Collecting instead

You can collect from Castleview Gardens, Ilford, IG1 3QF. Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling. Collection readiness is separate from dispatch and isn't tied to the 2pm dispatch time. We'll tell you what to bring when we confirm your collection. See [collection in Ilford](/number-plates-ilford).

## Where we deliver

We deliver by Royal Mail to addresses in Great Britain. If your address is in Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man, please contact us before ordering so we can tell you whether we can deliver there.

Royal Mail's next-working-day aim doesn't apply to some remote areas, such as parts of the Highlands and Islands of Scotland.

## If something goes wrong

If your parcel is late, missing or arrives damaged, contact us with your order number. A photo of the parcel and plate helps if you can take one. We'll look into it with Royal Mail. The plates remain at our risk until they are delivered to you. See [returns and cancellations](/returns) and [warranty and faulty plates](/warranty).
