# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies do the same job. We use them to make the site work and, if you agree, to understand how it is used.

## The types we use

| Type | What it does | Do we ask first? |
|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Makes the site and your basket work, keeps it secure and remembers your cookie choice | No. The law exempts these |
| **Analytics** | Helps us understand which pages are used, so we can improve the site | Yes |
| **Marketing** | Measures or personalises advertising | Yes |

Some cookies don't need your consent, such as those strictly necessary to provide something you ask for. The law also has a small number of narrow exceptions, for example for some basic statistical analytics where strict conditions are met and you can easily object. Advertising cookies always need consent. We've chosen to ask for your consent before setting any analytics or marketing cookies. See the [ICO's guidance on the exceptions](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/).

## Your choices

- When you first visit, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies, or choose which categories to allow.
- You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may then not work properly.

Rejecting non-essential cookies won't stop you using the site or placing an order.

Where a cookie involves personal information, see our [privacy policy](/privacy). For general guidance, see the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/online/cookies/).
