# Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to order number plates from us. If you find any part of the website difficult to use, including the plate builder, checkout or providing your documents, contact us and we'll help you order another way.

## Ordering with help

Contact us and tell us what you need. We can talk you through the builder, help you place your order and explain how to provide your documents. Our email is [info@replacementplates.uk](mailto:info@replacementplates.uk), our telephone number is 020 3576 6603 and our WhatsApp number is +44 7754 185366.

## What we're doing

We aim to build our pages so that they work with keyboards, screen readers and browser zoom, use clear headings and plain language, and don't rely on colour alone. The site has not yet had a formal accessibility audit, and some parts, such as the plate builder, may be harder to use than others.

## Tell us about a problem

If something doesn't work for you, tell us which page it's on and what happened. We'll try to fix it and help you in the meantime.

If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service.
