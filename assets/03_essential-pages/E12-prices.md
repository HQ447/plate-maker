# Number Plate Prices

Prices are for **plates**: the first price column is for **one plate** (front or rear), and the second is for a **front and rear pair** (two plates). There is no pair discount.

| Style | One plate (front or rear) | Front and rear pair (two plates) |
|---|---:|---:|
| [Standard](/standard-number-plates) | £12.49 | £24.98 |
| [3D gel](/3d-number-plates) | £19.95 | £39.90 |

## Delivery charges

Standard Royal Mail First Class delivery is £3 on orders under £15 and free on orders of £15 or more. Upgrade to Royal Mail Tracked 24 for an additional £2. Royal Mail delivery times are aims, not guarantees.

| Goods order value | Royal Mail First Class | Royal Mail Tracked 24 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Under £15 | £3 | £5 total delivery charge |
| £15 or more | Free | £2 total delivery charge |

An order of exactly £15 qualifies for free First Class. If you choose to collect from Castleview Gardens, Ilford, IG1 3QF, no delivery charge applies.

### Examples with delivery

| Order | Goods | Royal Mail First Class | Royal Mail Tracked 24 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| One Standard plate | £12.49 | £15.49 in total | £17.49 in total |
| Standard pair | £24.98 | £24.98 in total (free delivery) | £26.98 in total |

The delivery charge that applies to your order is shown next to our prices and in your basket, with a running total that includes it. Royal Mail Tracked 24 is optional and shown separately. If you choose to collect your plates from Ilford, no delivery charge applies.
