# How It Works

## 1. Build your plate

Enter your registration, choose front, rear or a pair, and pick your style and size. The builder shows a preview and the price. Not sure which style? See the [price list](/prices) or start with [standard number plates](/standard-number-plates).

## 2. Place your order and provide your documents

We need to check your identity and your entitlement to the registration. See [documents you need](/documents-you-need) for the accepted documents. We'll tell you how to provide them when you order.

## 3. We check, then make and supply your plates

We don't supply road-use plates until our required checks are complete. Once they are, your plates are made to your order. Road-use plates carry our name and postcode and the British Standard number. You can cancel for a full refund at any time before production starts; see [returns and cancellations](/returns).

## 4. Delivery or collection

- **Delivery:** Standard Royal Mail First Class delivery is £3 on orders under £15 and free on orders of £15 or more. Upgrade to Royal Mail Tracked 24 for an additional £2. Royal Mail delivery times are aims, not guarantees. If your order and checks are complete before 2pm on a working weekday, we aim to dispatch the same day. See [delivery](/delivery).
- **Collection:** from Castleview Gardens, Ilford, IG1 3QF. Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling. See [collection in Ilford](/number-plates-ilford).

Full walkthrough: [how to replace a number plate in the UK](/guides/how-to-replace-a-number-plate-in-the-uk).
