# Areas We Cover

## Collect in East London

Our one collection location is **Castleview Gardens, Ilford, IG1 3QF**. Ready within 3 hours (contact us via WhatsApp to confirm same-day collection). Please confirm before travelling.

We deliver by Royal Mail across Great Britain. Our only collection point is in Ilford; customers elsewhere can choose delivery or arrange collection from Ilford. See [delivery and dispatch](/delivery) for charges and timings.
